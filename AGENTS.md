# Project guidance

## Design language

Use the local Apple Design Skill at `.design-rules/SKILL.md` as the standing
design language for this project. For any UI/UX review or implementation,
follow its design-improvement process and consult the relevant Human Interface
Guidelines references. Keep the product accessible, responsive, and
platform-familiar while giving it a distinctive product-specific point of view.

The `.design-rules/` directory is intentionally local and ignored by Git.
